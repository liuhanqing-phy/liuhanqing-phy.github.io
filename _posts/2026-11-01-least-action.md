“I believe in the principle of least action.”

The familiar name is the **principle of least action**. More precisely, the physical path makes the action stationary:

$$
\delta S = 0.
$$

For a trajectory $$q(t)$$, the action is

$$
S[q] = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} L\bigl(q(t), \dot q(t), t\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}t.
$$

A vanishing first variation does not necessarily imply a minimum. “Stationary action” is the more general statement — and a useful reminder to look beyond the name.
